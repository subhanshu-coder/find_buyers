Made for considered outreach

HomeScout does not bulk-send messages or invent personal email addresses. Use accurate sender details, include a valid business postal address and an opt-out method in commercial email, and honor opt-out requests.

WHAT YOU NEED

Connect the services

FoursquareFinds retailer listings. Add FOURSQUARE_API_KEY to the server’s private .env file.

Snov.ioLooks up public business contacts. Add SNOV_API_USER_ID and SNOV_API_SECRET to .env.

ResendOptional email delivery. Add RESEND_API_KEY and a verified SENDER_EMAIL. Keep all secrets on the server; never put them in browser code.
