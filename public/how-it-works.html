Made for considered outreach

Review each address, personalize your message, use accurate sender details, include a valid business postal address and an opt-out method in commercial email, and honor opt-out requests. Public OSM data can be incomplete or stale; verify it on the retailer’s site before sending.

WHAT YOU NEED

Connect the services

FoursquareFinds retailer listings. Add FOURSQUARE_API_KEY to the Render service environment.

OpenStreetMapSearches public shop listings and contact tags with Nominatim and Overpass. Shared public services may be busy and do not contain every business.

Gmail SMTPInstall Nodemailer through npm install. Add GMAIL_USER and GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD in Render. Use a Google App Password with 2-Step Verification enabled; never put it in browser code.

Google SheetsInstall the included Gmail sent-mail logger from the Sheet sync guide. After authorization, it logs matching sent messages to your linked spreadsheet tab.
