Made for considered outreach

Email lookups may use Snov.io account credits. Review each address, personalize your message, use accurate sender details, include a valid business postal address and an opt-out method in commercial email, and honor opt-out requests.

WHAT YOU NEED

Connect the services

FoursquareFinds retailer listings. Add FOURSQUARE_API_KEY to Vercel Environment Variables.

Snov.ioLooks up public business contacts. Add SNOV_API_USER_ID and SNOV_API_SECRET to Vercel Environment Variables.

ResendOptional email delivery. Add RESEND_API_KEY and a verified SENDER_EMAIL. Keep all secrets on the server; never put them in browser code.
