WHAT YOU NEED

Connect the services

FoursquareFinds retailer listings. Add FOURSQUARE_API_KEY to Vercel Environment Variables.

ApolloSearches likely buyer roles and enriches matching people for work email addresses. Enrichment may use Apollo credits. Add APOLLO_API_KEY as a server-only Vercel Secret with People API Search and People Enrichment access.

ResendOptional email delivery. Add RESEND_API_KEY and a verified SENDER_EMAIL. Keep all secrets on the server; never put them in browser code.

Google SheetsInstall the included Gmail sent-mail logger from the Sheet sync guide. After authorization, it logs matching sent messages to your linked spreadsheet tab.
