Made for considered outreach

Review each address, personalize your message, use accurate sender details, include a valid business postal address and an opt-out method in commercial email, and honor opt-out requests. Public OSM data can be incomplete or stale; verify it on the retailer’s site before sending.

WHAT YOU NEED

Connect the services

Foursquare PlacesAdd FOURSQUARE_API_KEY in Render to find business listings and website URLs. OpenStreetMap is also queried for public listings.

Snov.ioAdd both SNOV_API_USER_ID and SNOV_API_SECRET in Render. Snov.io’s current docs say prospect profile search costs 1 credit per unique request; Email Finder costs 1 credit for each valid or unknown email it returns. Account plan and balance apply. Free-plan accounts may need to request API test access from Snov.io.

Public website lookupChecks a business website and a small number of same-site contact pages for visible email addresses. Results are not verified and many businesses publish no email.

OpenStreetMap + FoursquareOpenStreetMap may also list public email and phone tags.

GmailHomeScout opens Gmail compose with your draft prefilled. Sign in to Gmail in your browser, review the message, and send it yourself. No SMTP password is needed.

Google SheetsInstall the included Gmail sent-mail logger from the Sheet sync guide. After authorization, it logs matching sent messages to your linked spreadsheet tab.
