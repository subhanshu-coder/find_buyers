Made for considered outreach

Review each address, personalize your message, use accurate sender details, include a valid business postal address and an opt-out method in commercial email, and honor opt-out requests. Foursquare listings and Snov.io contact data can be incomplete or stale; verify details before sending.

WHAT YOU NEED

Connect the services

Foursquare PlacesAdd FOURSQUARE_API_KEY in Render to find business listings, listing emails, and website URLs. Foursquare controls the result cap and account plan.

Snov.ioAdd both SNOV_API_USER_ID and SNOV_API_SECRET in Render. Snov.io searches available prospect pages and sends names to Email Finder in provider-supported batches. Snov.io account access, API rate limits, plan restrictions, and credits apply; check your Snov.io dashboard for current billing.

GmailHomeScout opens Gmail compose with your draft prefilled. Sign in to Gmail in your browser, review the message, and send it yourself. No SMTP password is needed.

Google SheetsInstall the included Gmail sent-mail logger from the Sheet sync guide. After authorization, it logs matching sent messages to your linked spreadsheet tab.
