What it reads and writes

The logger checks Gmail Sent for subjects generated by HomeScout, such as “A thoughtful fit for Votive candle holders — Store Name.” It writes only matching sent emails to the four visible columns on the linked tab. A hidden helper column stores Gmail message IDs to prevent duplicate rows. The script does not send emails; you review and send each draft yourself. Keep the suggested product and company in the subject so they can be recorded correctly.
